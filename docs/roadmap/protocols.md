For a transparent bounded-observation planning calculation, use probability separation 0.20. The Hoeffding count is the square of the sum of square roots of the natural logarithms of reciprocal alpha and beta, divided by twice the square of 0.20. It is 394 after rounding up. Allocate 899 independent observations. At 899 observations the two-sided Dvoretzky-Kiefer-Wolfowitz error at beta is below 0.068. This supports only endpoints with that separation and those assumptions. It does not establish power for the retained 0.005 noninferiority margins, time ratios, rare errors, dependence or censoring. For paired log-time ratios, the pilot must supply an upper variance bound before an independent complete-gate power calculation chooses confirmation size. A normal planning example uses paired log standard deviation at most 0.40, a true ratio of 0.80 and an acceptance upper bound of 0.90. Square the sum of the standard normal quantiles at one minus alpha and one minus beta. Multiply by the square of 0.40 and divide by the square of the natural logarithm of 0.90 divided by 0.80. The rounded planning count is 522. Thus 899 observations cover this example only. Validate its distribution and complete-gate power independently before use. Never use a pilot effect estimate to reduce the required effect or change the primary.

## Analytic release error bound, owned by D28.c3a
This proof controls false statistical labels, not the probability that the tools are correct. Exact semantic errors have no statistical allowance. Assume each elementary test has a valid super-uniform p-value under its own null, at every allocated attempt and look. Optional stopping is allowed only with a valid anytime test or the sealed look allocation. Dependence between tests, categories, routes and looks is arbitrary.
For category c, let A equal 0.8 times 0.04 divided by 19. A category conjunction is false only if at least one required component null is true. Choose one such true null j before outcomes. The intersection-union test accepts the conjunction only if it rejects j. Thus its false-label probability is at most A, irrespective of the number of required components or their dependence. No component count correction is needed for this conjunction. This is not permission to publish each component as a separate label.
For a fixed ordered tier sequence, choose the first false tier. Any false later tier requires rejection of that first false tier. The fixed-sequence family therefore has error at most its allocated level. If routes, attempts or repeated looks offer alternatives, assign nonnegative weights before outcomes, with total at most one. The union bound gives error at most the sum of their allocated levels, which is at most A. Weights and failed or unused slots cannot be recycled. Data-dependent switching without this allocation invalidates the bound.
Optional atomic labels use the separate category budget B equal 0.2 times 0.04 divided by 19. Holm controls their family under arbitrary dependence: if any true null is rejected, the smallest p-value among the m0 true nulls is at most B divided by m0. The union bound over these m0 tests is at most B. Preallocated route, attempt and look weights split this budget further; they do not add a new budget. Optional results cannot rescue a failed required conjunction.
Each category validation family has budget V equal 0.01 divided by 19. Apply the same valid local tests and union accounting to all validation decisions that can authorize a label. A diagnostic simulation does not prove p-value validity. Sum the scientific and validation families over 19 categories: 19 times (A plus B plus V) equals 0.04 plus 0.01, which equals 0.05. Therefore the probability of at least one false statistical release label is at most 0.05 under the stated test-validity assumptions, without an independence assumption. This is a per-release statement. Cross-release claims require their own preallocated ledger.
Joint power is separate. Allocate total failure probability at most 0.2 across all required components under the sealed beneficial alternative; the union bound then gives joint power at least 0.8. Dependence cannot justify multiplying marginal powers. If the marginal validity, source custody, route allocation or alternative model fails, the bound is not established and affected labels remain OPEN.
